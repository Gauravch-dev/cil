CURRENT - ELECTRICITY READINGS TEMPLATES

Blank CSV template
Use the header-only file for your actual meter readings. Add one row per minute.
Supply at least eight days of history, ending with a recent complete interval.

Required columns and units
Datetime: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:00, in the timezone selected on the upload form.
Global_active_power: kW (not cumulative kWh or a monthly total).
Global_reactive_power: kVAR.
Voltage: V.
Global_intensity: A.
Sub_metering_1: Wh per minute.
Sub_metering_2: Wh per minute.
Sub_metering_3: Wh per minute.

Filled sample
SYNTHETIC-electricity-readings-8-days.csv contains 11,520 invented minute readings
for 1-8 January 2026 in UTC. It demonstrates the format and supports a clearly labelled sample-date forecast.
Its extra Data_source column marks every row synthetic_sample. Choose Use filled
sample, then Generate sample forecast. The model uses the original sample dates
and your household settings guide the rules and schedule. The app blocks this
file from actual current household forecasting.
It is not real meter data, an accuracy result or evidence of household savings.

Using your file
Choose Use my electricity readings, select your CSV and its timezone, and click
Check my readings. Review coverage, completeness and freshness. A current estimate
requires actual recent readings and a compatible forecasting service.

The date range limit is eight to 60 days; the file limit is 10 MB. The latest
complete interval must end within the last 30 minutes. Leave missing values blank.
Do not change the sample dates to make invented readings look current.
